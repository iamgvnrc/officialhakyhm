      <div class="about-inner">
        <div class="about-heading">
          <p class="eyebrow">About Me</p>
          <h2>WHERE IT STARTED</h2>
        </div>

        <div class="about-content">
          <div class="about-copy">
            <p class="about-lead">
              I started making music during a time in my life when I needed help,
              but didn't know who I could really vent to.
            </p>

            <p>
              I was going through house evictions, uncertainty, and the normal
              struggles that come with life. There were things I was carrying
              inside that I didn't always know how to explain to somebody else.
              Music became the place where I could finally let those feelings out.
            </p>

            <p>
              What started as a way for me to express myself became something
              bigger. I realized there were other people out there dealing with
              their own struggles, feeling like nobody understood them or like
              they had to face everything by themselves.
            </p>

            <p>
              That's the message behind my music: <strong>YOU ARE NEVER ALONE.</strong>
              Even when times get hard and it feels like nobody is there, your
              story isn't over. I want my music to remind people that they can
              keep going, keep believing, and make it through whatever they're
              facing.
            </p>
          </div>

          <div class="about-stats">
