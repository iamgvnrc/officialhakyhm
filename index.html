ARTIST BIO

HAKYHM

Hakyhm is a singer and songwriter from Findlay, Ohio whose work blends Pop, R&B, and Hip Hop. With a sound that moves between melodic vocals, emotional storytelling, and genre-bending production, Hakyhm continues to carve out a distinct identity within today’s evolving music landscape.

His growing catalog has surpassed 1 MILLION+ STREAMS, connecting with listeners through authentic records that reflect both personal experiences and universal emotions. With an extensive catalog of music and visual content, Hakyhm is steadily building momentum as an independent artist while continuing to expand his reach and creative vision.
